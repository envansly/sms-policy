Eric VanSlyke — SMS Privacy Policy

Brand: Eric VanSlyke (Sole Proprietor)
Last updated: September 15, 2026

This privacy policy belongs to Eric VanSlyke, sole proprietor, and covers the SMS messaging program operated by Eric VanSlyke from the number (765) 633-8696.

Who receives messages

Messages are sent only to individuals who have signed up for Eric VanSlyke alerts by texting the opt-in keyword from their own mobile phone. No one is enrolled without taking that step. The program is small: at present the only enrolled user is the operator, Eric VanSlyke.

How consent is given

Consent is given by texting START to (765) 633-8696 from the mobile phone that should receive alerts. The sign-up instructions and full disclosure are published at https://envansly.github.io/sms-policy/optin.html. A confirmation message is sent when a user signs up. No numbers are added by any other method.

What the messages are

Messages are operational notifications about Eric VanSlyke's own email inbox: short summaries of items that need his decision, and status updates from scheduled checks. Messages are informational and transactional. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Message frequency is low and variable. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Information collected

The only information collected is the mobile phone number a user texts from when signing up, together with the date and time of the opt-in and any later STOP request. No names, addresses, or other personal data are requested or stored.

How information is used

Mobile numbers are used only to deliver the alerts the user signed up for and to honor opt-out requests. They are used for no other purpose.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Eric VanSlyke does not sell, rent, trade, or share any information with any third party for marketing. Message content is transmitted through Twilio, the messaging provider, solely to deliver the message. No other disclosure occurs, except where required by law.

Data retention

Message content is retained only as long as needed to operate the program and is deleted thereafter. The operator may delete stored data at any time.

Security

Access to the system is restricted to Eric VanSlyke through authenticated credentials. Messages are transmitted using the messaging provider's standard secure channels.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help.

Children

This program is not directed to children and no information is knowingly collected from anyone under 13.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be reflected in this document with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Eric VanSlyke by replying HELP to any message.
